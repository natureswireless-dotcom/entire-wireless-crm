# Entire Wireless ISP CRM v9.6

## Business plan and subscriber classification

- Adds a standard **Business Internet** service plan at **$100.00/month** plus a **$10.00/month modem lease** ($110.00 standard monthly total).
- Existing residential **Unlimited Internet** plan remains $85.00/month plus $10.00/month modem lease.
- Adds a required **Customer Type** classification: Residential or Business.
- New subscribers can be classified during account creation.
- Existing subscribers default to Residential and can be changed to Business from Edit Customer.
- Customer type is shown on customer profiles and the Customers list.
- Customer type is included in customer CSV exports and printable customer reports.
- Preserves no-plan customers, modem promotions, Stripe onboarding, Zoho Books sync, account-number generation, customer editing/deletion, and all prior v9.5 features.
