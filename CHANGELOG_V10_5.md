# Entire Wireless CRM v10.5 — Tablet/iPad Service
- Adds Tablet / iPad Unlimited Data plan at $45/month.
- Imports paid WooCommerce tablet/iPad orders.
- Requires the 15-digit IMEI supplied at checkout and assigns it to the customer equipment record.
- Stores physical SIM vs eSIM choice.
- Adds $15 activation fee and $5 physical-SIM fee when applicable to the imported first invoice.
- Adds Tablet/iPad Services section to customer profiles.
- Preserves an existing Residential/Business subscription when an existing customer adds a tablet plan.
- Includes active tablet services in recurring monthly billing.
- Preserves v10.4 customer onboarding and all prior CRM features.
