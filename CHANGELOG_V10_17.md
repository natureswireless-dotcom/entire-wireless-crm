# Entire Wireless ISP CRM v10.17 — Customer Referral Program

- Adds a Customer Referral Program to subscriber records.
- Each qualified new-customer referral awards the referring customer one free modem-rental month (normally $10).
- Rewards accumulate: six qualified referrals produce six free modem-rental months.
- Adds a unique referral code for every existing and future customer.
- Staff can record a referral during customer creation using referral code or account number, or from the referring customer's Referral Program page.
- Referrals begin as Pending and must be qualified by staff before a reward is issued.
- Adds a referral reward ledger for earned and redeemed months.
- Recurring billing automatically applies one available referral month to waive the modem lease.
- Existing modem-rental promotions take priority; referral months remain banked until promotional free months are exhausted.
- Customer profile displays referral code, available free months, value, referring customer, and referral history.
- Permanent test-customer deletion now cleans up referral records and reward-ledger entries safely.
- Preserves all v10.16.2 ThingSpace diagnostics and prior CRM functionality.
