# Entire Wireless ISP CRM v10.0

- Redesigned generated invoice/statement PDFs to closely match the supplied Entire Wireless Word bill layout.
- Page 1 now includes invoice number, the customer's live CRM account number, billing/contact address, billing summary, Manage Your Account panel, portal QR code, and payment stub.
- Page 2 contains detailed charges using the actual invoice line items and the service plan assigned in the CRM. No legacy/template service plans are hard-coded.
- Page 3 contains tax/detail information and important customer/account information.
- Residential and Business plans continue to come only from the CRM plan table. Current seeded plans remain Unlimited Internet ($85 + $10 modem lease) and Business Internet ($100 + $10 modem lease).
- Billing address is used when available; otherwise the service address is used.
- Existing invoice, Stripe, Zoho, Sales & Growth, customer classification, account suffix, promotion, and recurring billing functionality is preserved.
