# Entire Wireless ISP CRM v10.3

- Automatically generates the 36-Month Internet Service Agreement after a paid WooCommerce service order is accepted.
- Auto-fills Service Order / Customer Information from CRM/WooCommerce customer, plan, billing, service address, dates, order number, and assigned equipment when available.
- Auto-fills Customer Acknowledgment and Signature from the WooCommerce authorized signer/electronic signature and acceptance timestamp.
- Saves the generated DOCX in the customer's Files/Documents section as document type `Service Order/Customer Information`.
- Preserves the original agreement template and all v10.2 WooCommerce, billing, messaging, accounting, inventory, and Sales & Growth functionality.
