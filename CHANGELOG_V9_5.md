# Entire Wireless ISP CRM v9.5

- Adds an Edit Customer screen on every customer account.
- Edit account number, first and last name, company, email, phone, service address, billing address, city, state, ZIP, service plan, billing day, contract start, and contract length.
- Customer edits are recorded in the audit log.
- Zoho Books linked contacts are updated automatically when Zoho is the active accounting provider.
- Zoho sync failures do not undo the CRM edit and are logged for review.
- Existing invoices, payments, equipment assignments, documents, notes, and billing history are preserved.
- Header updated to v9.5.
