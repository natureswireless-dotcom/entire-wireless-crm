# Entire Wireless ISP CRM v10.15 — Internal Collections

- Added Entire Wireless Internal Collections center.
- Lists customers with past-due CRM invoice balances.
- Adds preconfigured Past-Due, Second Collection, and Final Collection notices.
- Auto-populates customer name, mailing address, account number, current past-due balance, and date.
- Generates printable PDF letters using the supplied Entire Wireless #9 double-window letterhead artwork.
- Supports manual email delivery using existing CRM SMTP configuration.
- Records generated/email history and employee audit events.
- Adds Internal Collections shortcut to customer profiles.
- Additive collection_letters table only; existing CRM data and features are preserved.
