# V9.5 Account Number & Header Update

- New subscriber account numbers are now generated automatically.
- Format is exactly 9 digits followed by `-0001`, beginning with `100000001-0001` and incrementing to the next available number.
- Account numbers are no longer manually entered during customer creation.
- Existing customer account numbers are preserved.
- Account numbers are read-only on the Edit Customer screen.
- Header displays `Entire Wireless ISP CRM v9.5`.
- Entire Wireless logo is displayed immediately before the CRM name in the top header.
