# V9.5 No Service Plan Update

- New subscribers may now be created with **No Service Plan**.
- Existing customers may be edited and their current service plan removed by selecting **No Service Plan**.
- Removing a service plan deletes the customer's active subscription record, which prevents plan-based recurring billing from running for that customer.
- Customers without a service plan remain valid CRM customer records and can still retain contact details, equipment, invoices, payments, notes, documents, and accounting links.
- A service plan can later be assigned again from Edit Customer.
- Preserves the V9.5 automatic 9-digit `-0001` account-number format and V9.5 header/logo branding.
