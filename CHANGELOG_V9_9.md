# Entire Wireless ISP CRM v9.9

This release restores the Sales & Growth module that was unintentionally omitted from the v9.6 Business Plan -> v9.8 lineage.

Restored Sales & Growth features include:
- Sales & Growth dashboard
- Lead management and lead stages
- Internal sales tasks and follow-ups
- Referral/partner tracking
- Rural coverage/address research
- Sales reports and spending tracking
- Launch setup and 30-day launch checklist
- Website Check Availability / inquiry workflow
- Growth CSV exports
- Automatic growth-status synchronization from CRM invoice/payment activity

Preserved from the latest CRM:
- Residential and Business customer classification
- Residential account suffix -0001
- Business account suffix -0002
- Residential and Business service plans
- Stripe onboarding/card payment workflow
- Recurring 36-month billing behavior
- Zoho Books integration
- Six-month free modem rental promotion
- Configurable customer CSV export, including service-agent notes
- Customer signup date and Member Since YYYY
- Existing customer, billing, equipment, reporting, portal, and carrier-control functionality
