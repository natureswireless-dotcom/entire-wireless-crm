# V9.5 Customer CSV Export Update

- Added CSV export from the Customers page.
- Export includes account number, customer name, company, email, phone, service and billing addresses, city/state/ZIP, account/service status, service plan, billing day, contract dates/term, and created date.
- Export follows the current customer search filter; clearing search exports the full list.
- CSV is UTF-8 with BOM for improved Microsoft Excel compatibility.
- Expanded customer search to include company and phone.
